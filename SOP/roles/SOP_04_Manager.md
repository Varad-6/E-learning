# 👔 Standard Operating Procedure: Manager Role

**Internal Code**: `MANAGER`  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Effective Date**: 09-Jul-2026  
**Audience**: Managers, Department Heads, Team Leaders  
**Scope**: Department Progress, Deadline Auditing, N:1 Feedback  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP guides Department Heads and Team Managers on how to monitor their team's learning completions, track quiz attempt performances, and give feedback to learners.

---

## 2. Capabilities & Constraints
*   **Scope**: Access is scoped strictly to employees belonging to the manager's assigned department. Managers cannot see data from other departments or change global settings.
*   **Read Access**: Dashboards, quiz scores, course completion lists, and learning notes.
*   **Write Access**: Giving employee feedback and requesting deadline extensions.
*   **Restricted**: Cannot modify course content or alter user roles.

---

## 3. Team Progress Auditing

### 3.1 Weekly Roster Review
1. Navigate to your **Dashboard**.
2. View the **Team Progress Matrix**:
    *   **In Progress**: Employees active in courses.
    *   **Completed**: Course achievements.
    *   **At Risk / Locked**: Employees who have missed or are close to missing deadlines.
3. Click on any employee name to view their progress percentage per course module.

### 3.2 Requesting Deadline Extensions (Escalation)
If an employee needs more time:
1. On the employee's locked course entry in the manager dashboard, click **Request Extension**.
2. Enter the justification and proposed extension date.
3. The request is sent to the Course Manager or System Admin for override approval.

---

## 4. Giving Feedback (N:1 Feedback System) [PLANNED]
Once an employee completes a course or takes a quiz, the manager should submit an evaluation:
1. Navigate to **Dashboard -> Team Feedback -> Select Employee**.
2. Click **Submit Performance Feedback**.
3. Complete the form:
    *   **Rating** (1-5 Star)
    *   **Quiz Review Comments** (Targeting questions answered incorrectly)
    *   **Areas of Improvement**
4. Click **Submit**. The employee is notified, and the feedback is logged to their training history.
