# 📖 Standard Operating Procedure: Employee (Learner) Role

**Internal Code**: `EMPLOYEE`  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Effective Date**: 09-Jul-2026  
**Audience**: All employees and company learners  
**Scope**: Course Enrollment, Player Navigation, Notes, Quizzes  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP guides employees on how to access their dashboard, complete training materials, use note-taking capabilities, and take quizzes to ensure compliance and upskilling.

---

## 2. Page Navigation

### 2.1 First-Time Login
1. Open the Kiezen ELMS link.
2. Click **Learner Portal**.
3. Input your email and temporary password received from HR.
4. Complete OTP verification sent to your email.
5. Create a new secure password.

### 2.2 Finding & Enrolling in Courses
*   **Dashboard Page**: Browse all available department courses, view their code/duration/difficulty, and self-enroll by clicking **Start Course**. If you are already enrolled, the button will be locked and labeled "Already Enrolled". The sidebar displays your completed courses and digital verified credentials.
*   **My Courses Page**: Access your active enrolled curriculum (all courses with progress < 100%), click **Resume Course** to return to your lessons, and inspect your recent session activity in the sidebar.

---

## 3. Using the Course Player

### 3.1 Course Screen Controls
*   **Left Sidebar**: List of modules. Checkmarks indicate completed sections.
*   **Center Area**: Video player, text viewer, or PDF document reader.
*   **Notes Panel (Right side)**: Place to type personal study notes. Click **Save Notes** to link them to the module.

### 3.2 Access Limits & Deadlines
*   Check the **Due Date** on your dashboard.
*   **Warning**: If the deadline passes before completion, the course locks automatically. You must contact your manager to request an unlock override.

### 3.3 Taking Quizzes
1. When you reach the final module page, click **Take Quiz**.
2. Answer the MCQs.
3. Click **Submit Quiz**. The system instantly displays your score, pass/fail status, and reviewer explanations.
