# 📑 Features & Capabilities: Enrollment & Assignment Flows

**Version**: 1.0  
**Effective Date**: 09-Jul-2026  

---

## 1. Enrollment States
*   `not_started`: Enrolled but course player not opened.
*   `in_progress`: Modules viewed, progress updated.
*   `completed`: All module items viewed and quizzes passed.
*   `locked`: Access deadline missed.
